BRAND IDENTITY • SOCIAL MEDIA DESIGN

Socially Yours. A visual system built for social.

A visual identity and social media design system developed to give Socially Yours a recognizable, flexible, and contemporary visual language.

CLIENT Socially Yours
SCOPE Brand Identity + Social
ROLE Designer
DELIVERABLES Identity, templates, social system

PROJECT SNAPSHOT

Challenge

Create consistency across fast-moving social content without making every post feel the same.

Response

Build a flexible identity, layout rules, and reusable content components around one recognisable visual voice.

Outcome

A system that lets the brand publish with clarity while keeping space for variety and personality.

01 / OVERVIEW

Building consistency without making the brand feel predictable.

Socially Yours needed a visual language that could stretch across different types of social content while still feeling like one recognizable brand.

The work focused on establishing the foundations of the identity first, then applying those foundations to social media graphics.

02 / THE IDENTITY

A system, not just a logo.

The identity was developed as a collection of visual elements that could work together across different applications.

03 / VISUAL EXPLORATION

The details create the personality.

Supporting elements were developed alongside the core identity to give the brand more room to communicate, experiment, and stay visually distinctive.

04 / SOCIAL APPLICATION

From identity to content.

The visual system was then translated into social media content, giving individual posts their own character while maintaining a recognizable connection to the wider brand.

05 / DESIGN APPROACH

“Strong social design doesn't mean making every post loud. It means creating enough visual consistency that the brand is recognizable before the audience reads the handle.”

THE FINAL SYSTEM

One identity. Many expressions.
