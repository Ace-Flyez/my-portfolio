BRAND IDENTITY

Serenity. Identity shaped around calm.

A visual identity exploration built around creating a distinctive and cohesive brand language through logo, colour, typography, and supporting visual elements.

CLIENT Serenity
SCOPE Brand Identity
ROLE Designer
DELIVERABLES Identity, art direction, applications

PROJECT SNAPSHOT

Challenge

Express calm and clarity in an identity that still feels intentional and distinctive.

Response

Pair a restrained mark with a gentle palette, measured typography, and visual rules that make the mood tangible.

Outcome

A cohesive identity that carries the same calm character across every touchpoint.

01 / OVERVIEW

Turning an idea into a visual language.

The Serenity identity was developed through a combination of visual exploration and structured identity design.

The process considered the relationship between the logo, typography, colour, imagery, and supporting elements so that the final identity could feel like a complete system.

02 / LOGO EXPLORATION

Finding the form.

The identity began with exploration. Sketches, construction, variations, and refinement were used to develop the final visual mark.

03 / PRIMARY IDENTITY

One mark. Multiple expressions.

The final identity was organized into a primary mark and supporting variations, creating flexibility while preserving recognition.

04 / VISUAL SYSTEM

The identity beyond the logo.

Typography, colour, photography, and supporting visual decisions extend the identity into a broader visual system.

05 / APPLICATION

Seeing the identity in context.

Mockups provide a final view of how the identity can move from presentation into practical brand applications.

06 / THE FINAL IDENTITY

A complete visual system, brought together.

DESIGN APPROACH

“The strongest identities don't need to shout. They create a feeling people remember.”
