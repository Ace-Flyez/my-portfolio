About Me

I'm a passionate creative with a background in graphic design, specializing in brand identity, social media design, and photo manipulation. Over the years, I have developed a unique style focused on originality and visual impact.

Driven by curiosity and a love for digital experiences, I am now learning full-stack web development. This journey allows me to merge design and functionality into interactive, user-friendly web interfaces.

When I am not designing or coding, I explore ideas, learn new tools, and work on projects that challenge creativity and logic.
