FOOD BRAND • VISUAL DESIGN

Cassie Delish. Making food look irresistible.

A collection of brand and social media design work created to give Cassie Delish a distinctive and appetizing visual presence across its customer-facing materials.

CLIENT Cassie Delish
SCOPE Brand + Social Design
ROLE Designer
DELIVERABLES Identity, menu design, social templates

PROJECT SNAPSHOT

Challenge

Make a food brand feel as tempting and memorable on-screen as the menu is in real life.

Response

Shape an appetite-led identity and translate it into practical menu, campaign, and social content formats.

Outcome

A visual system that makes information easy to use and the food impossible to overlook.

01 / OVERVIEW

Design that makes the menu part of the experience.

Cassie Delish combines food presentation with social-first communication. The design work needed to make information easy to understand while keeping the food itself visually compelling.

The project includes branded presentation material and multiple social media menu and promotional designs.

02 / BRAND PRESENTATION

Establishing the visual direction.

The branding presentation establishes the visual foundation used to guide the wider design work.

03 / MENU DESIGN

Turning information into appetite.

Menu designs balance food imagery, pricing, item names, and promotional information without allowing the layout to become difficult to navigate.

04 / SOCIAL APPLICATION

Built to stop the scroll.

Promotional graphics translate the food offering into visual content designed for quick recognition on social platforms.

DESIGN APPROACH

“When the product already looks good, the design should make people want to experience it.”

05 / SELECTED WORK

A visual system made to be useful.
