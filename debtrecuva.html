CAMPAIGN VISUALS • SOCIAL MEDIA

DebtRecuva. Making recovery feel human.

A visual communication system built to present debt recovery with clarity, confidence, and a more contemporary visual language.

CLIENT DebtRecuva
SCOPE Campaign Visuals + Social Media
ROLE Visual Designer
DELIVERABLES Campaign art direction, social graphics

PROJECT SNAPSHOT

Challenge

Communicate a serious financial service in a way that feels credible, human, and easy to engage with.

Response

Use calm visual direction, clear hierarchy, and a focused campaign system instead of financial noise.

Outcome

A more considered public face that makes complex recovery messaging easier to understand.

01 / OVERVIEW

Turning a serious subject into something people can actually engage with.

Debt recovery is naturally associated with paperwork, pressure, phone calls, and financial complexity. The visual direction needed to communicate the subject without falling into the familiar look of generic finance advertising.

The approach was therefore built around strong visual metaphors, realistic imagery, restrained colour, and clear editorial composition.

02 / VISUAL DIRECTION

Calm authority, not financial noise.

The visual language combines clean white space, controlled purple gradients, realistic photography, depth, and carefully placed supporting details.

03 / CAMPAIGN THINKING

The message starts with the visual.

Rather than simply placing copy over attractive backgrounds, each composition begins with an idea that can communicate the subject visually.

Objects, people, depth, lighting, scale, and composition are used deliberately so the viewer can understand the direction before reading every word.

04 / COMMUNICATION SYSTEM

Different messages. One visual language.

The system is flexible enough to support different DebtRecuva communication themes while keeping the overall brand presence recognisable.

05 / DESIGN APPROACH

Less decoration. More intention.

THE PRINCIPLE

Typography carries a significant part of the visual hierarchy, while imagery provides the emotional and conceptual layer.

Purple and lavender create the recognisable brand presence, while neutral surfaces keep the work grounded and readable. Small contrasting accents are used sparingly rather than allowing colour to become the entire design.

06 / THE RESULT

“Debt recovery doesn't have to look intimidating to feel professional.”

07 / FINAL VISUAL

A more considered face for a serious service.
