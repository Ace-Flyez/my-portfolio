BRAND IDENTITY

Valiant Fit. Built to feel strong.

A visual identity exploration for Valiant Fit, bringing together logo development, typography, colour, and supporting brand elements into one cohesive system.

CLIENT Valiant Fit
SCOPE Brand Identity
ROLE Designer
DELIVERABLES Identity, typography, brand applications

PROJECT SNAPSHOT

Challenge

Give a fitness brand a confident presence without relying on the usual visual clichés.

Response

Develop a distinctive mark, assertive typography, and a supporting visual language built around movement and strength.

Outcome

A complete identity system with enough range to work from first impression to everyday application.

01 / INTRODUCTION

Creating a visual identity with presence.

The Valiant Fit identity explores how typography, form, colour, and visual structure can come together to create a confident fitness brand.

The process moved from initial visual exploration into a more defined identity system, allowing the final mark and supporting elements to work together rather than functioning as isolated pieces.

02 / LOGO DEVELOPMENT

From idea to identity.

The identity development process explored the construction, proportions, and variations of the Valiant Fit mark.

03 / VISUAL LANGUAGE

More than the mark.

A recognizable identity needs more than a logo. Colour, typography, and supporting visual decisions help establish how the brand behaves across different applications.

04 / APPLICATION

Giving the identity somewhere to live.

The identity was presented through branded applications designed to show how the visual language could translate beyond the primary logo.

05 / THE SYSTEM

A complete identity, presented as one system.

DESIGN APPROACH

“A strong identity should be recognizable without needing to explain itself.”
